## Maintenance

- When a decision is made, first update the applicable requirement and contract; then adjust `TASKS.md` and `DIVERGENCES.md` in the same interaction.
- When implementation is complete, move its observable behavior to `CURRENT-STATE.md` and record coverage in [`../../REGRESSION-COVERAGE.md`](../../REGRESSION-COVERAGE.md). Remove only resolved or explicitly discarded items from this list, maintaining traceability through the task/divergence.
- Do not turn these proposals into current behavior by inference. Long-term strategy requirements remain in [`PRODUCT-REQUIREMENTS.md`](PRODUCT-REQUIREMENTS.md).
