## Summary

**Official summary:** Siri AI is a beta experience on supported Apple Intelligence hardware. Device and Siri languages must use the same supported language; this version supports English. Availability also depends on the Apple Account region. The dedicated Siri app synchronizes conversational history through iCloud.

For the current diagnostic, English is the primary device language and Siri uses English (United States). Correct settings and successful basic controls do not identify the cause of a private planner error. The published article does not define GenerativeError code 1010000.
