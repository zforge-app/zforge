# App Shortcuts

## Summary

App Shortcuts use App Intents to package common actions. The macOS-specific guidance states that App Shortcuts are unsupported on macOS, while App Intents actions are supported and can be combined into custom shortcuts in the Mac Shortcuts app. This platform-specific guidance takes precedence over assuming that generic examples of automatic App Shortcut phrases apply to Mac.

## Practical implications

Zforge must expose actions in the Mac Shortcuts editor and guide people to save a custom shortcut for invocation by name. Compilation of an AppShortcutsProvider is not proof of macOS support.
