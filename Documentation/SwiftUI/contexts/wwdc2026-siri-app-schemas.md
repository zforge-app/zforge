## Summary

**Official summary:** Apple describes entities, compatible schemas and contextual view associations for Siri in the 27 releases.

Generic intents are useful in Shortcuts and other surfaces. Siri actions require compatible schema contracts. Entity indexing or string queries resolve app content; view associations support references to visible items. Validate business logic first, then Shortcuts, Spotlight and actual Siri separately.
